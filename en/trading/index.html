Mohajer Mobin Steel coordinates inquiries for industrial and construction steel products. Grade, dimensions, quantity, mill source, inspection and delivery terms are assessed before a commercial offer.

Industrial steel sourcing enquiries

An effective procurement request specifies an exact grade, standard, dimensions, tonnage, destination, inspection requirements and timeframe. Prices from different sources should be compared using the same product and Incoterms basis.

Availability and the confirmed production source can only be established during the sourcing review. A published generic market figure is not a firm quotation.

Steel billets, rebar, plates and slabs

Our priority guides describe 3SP and 5SP steel billets, A2, A3 and A4 rebar, ST37 and ST52 steel plate and semi-finished steel slabs. Each product has different technical acceptance and pricing factors.

The buyer should supply the required mill certificate, engineering drawing or product standard as relevant.

Import enquiries and compliance considerations

Importing steel requires country-specific verification of customs classification, origin, technical standards, duties, transport and destination trade restrictions. Applicable rules can change, so the parties should review the requirements for the specific goods and route.

This is general import-process guidance, not a statement that Mohajer Mobin Steel holds particular import authorizations or can execute every import transaction. Any service capability must be confirmed directly with the trading team.

Steel export and commercial delivery

Review our steel export process for documentation, inspection and shipment considerations. A final agreement should identify the material specification, price basis, payment terms, risk transfer and named place of delivery.

For offers, send a technical and commercial RFQ to the official company email.

Formal enquiry and delivery conditions

Provide the grade, dimensions, specification, tonnage, destination and timeframe. Price and availability are determined on a transaction-specific basis.
